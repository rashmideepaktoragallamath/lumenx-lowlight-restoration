## 1. Problem understanding and strategy
**Challenge.** Severe under-exposure, sensor noise amplified by brightening, colour casts from mixed lights, non-uniform lighting (lamps and windows next to dark areas). Constraint: the hosted demo must answer in about 10 s on CPU.
**Method.** Image statistics drive every parameter (no fixed constants). A classical branch gives an illumination map and a conservative result. Zero-DCE++ gives a data-driven result and receives the illumination map as a 4th channel. A per-pixel blend and a no-reference quality score combine them. Static pipelines either clip highlights (DL) or amplify noise (classical).

## 2. Technical architecture
See `docs/architecture.png`.
### 2.1 Classical baseline
Guided-filter (LIME-style) illumination, single-scale Retinex, guided + bilateral denoise, CLAHE on Lab L, partial shades-of-gray white balance, saturation. Parameters come from `analysis.choose_params`: gamma from median luminance and bright fraction, denoise strength / guided eps / CLAHE clip from the Immerkaer noise estimate, CLAHE tile from local contrast, curve iterations from darkness class, blend threshold from illumination non-uniformity.
### 2.2 Deep learning
Zero-DCE++ (7 depthwise-separable convs, 8 curve iterations). Chosen because it is tiny (about 10k parameters), fast on CPU and can take extra input channels. Losses: spatial consistency, exposure, colour constancy, illumination smoothness, plus L1 and SSIM when paired data is used. Data: LOL our485, leakage-safe split 395 train / 66 val (scene-grouped) / 15 test, `scripts/prepare_data.py`.
### 2.3 Hybrid integration
Classical illumination L enters the network as channel 4 and defines the blend weight w = guided(sigmoid((theta - L)/0.08)): dark pixels use the network, lit pixels the classical output. Soft-min over {C, D, B} by a no-reference score (exposure, new clipping, colour cast, relative noise, entropy, contrast), then noise-damped detail transfer and a hue-preserving tone-map shoulder.

## Limitations and ethics
* Parameter policy and quality score are hand-written heuristics, not learned; the quality score was uncorrelated with PSNR on LOL.
* LOL is small (485 pairs, indoor); generalisation is checked only with no-reference metrics on DICM, LIME, ExDark. NIQE favours dark smooth images and is read with the other measures.
* Enhancement can reveal content in images that were dark on purpose (privacy, surveillance); do not use it to identify people without consent. It can hallucinate texture and must not be used as forensic evidence.
* Uploaded images are processed in memory and not stored by the app. Datasets are research-use (LOL (c) Wei et al. 2018) and are not redistributed here.
